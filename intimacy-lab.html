Care note

Why petroleum jelly is not a lube substitute

Petroleum jelly is oil-based and designed to create a barrier on external skin. That barrier is useful for dry or cracked skin, but it is not designed as an intimate lubricant.

Why it can be a poor choice

  • It does not rinse away easily, so residue can remain longer than expected.
  • It can trap moisture and bacteria, which may increase irritation risk.
  • It is not pH-balanced for sensitive intimate areas.
  • Oil-based products can weaken latex protection methods.

What to use instead

Choose products made specifically for intimate comfort. Water-based formulas are usually easier to clean, more bedroom-friendly, and better suited to sensitive care routines.

How to wear Aroma

Apply lightly to pulse points or use as a personal scent layer. Start small. A refined scent should sit close, not fill the entire room.

Fabric and skin care

Let oils settle before dressing and avoid direct application on delicate fabrics. Patch test if your skin is sensitive.

Amber bottle storage

Keep bottles closed and away from direct heat or sunlight. Amber packaging helps protect formula integrity, but storage still matters.
