Scent Room

Build a room that smells considered, not loud.

Pair Aroma oils, candlelight, and amber-bottled care like a small home counter: clean, private, and easy to repeat. This page is about atmosphere as much as comfort.

01

Aroma Point

Use Aroma 30ml as the first layer: one close scent note on skin before the room is prepared.

02

Candle Counter

Place the Signature Candle where it frames the shelf, not where it overwhelms the space.

03

Formula Pick

Choose by purpose: Classic for everyday smoothness, Mint for a cool finish, Painless for gentler ease.

04

Private Shelf

Keep bottles sealed, upright, and away from heat. The shelf should look intentional even when guests see it.
